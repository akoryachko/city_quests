# Family profile for city quests

Example. Your own profile goes in `family-profile.md` next to this file. It is
git-ignored, so it stays private. If it is missing, the `city-quest` skill
asks a few questions and creates it for you.

## Players
- Kid: 8-year-old girl. No name on the booklet; feminine wording. Speaks Dutch and Russian, so no transcription for Dutch words; in Dutch-speaking places she is the team's interpreter.
- Parents play along: co-op as one team against the city, with kid-vs-parent duels as side quests.
- Home city: Amsterdam (home-city campaigns start at Amsterdam Centraal).

## Booklet
- Language: Russian.
- Printed on a black-and-white printer (A4 landscape, cut into A5 cards) or read on a phone (quest.md).
- Parents usually haven't been to the city before, so the quest doubles as their guide.
- Anything that needs booking, reserving or a ride: explicit instructions on the parent pages (where, how step by step, cost, link).

## Likes
- Collecting stamps, stickers and other small collectables.
- JetLag: The Game style: challenges, curses, claiming territory, scaled down.
- Spotting historic details and the stories behind them; the parents want those stories per spot.
- Variety above all: mix challenge kinds and keep each in proportion.
- Measuring and simple maths in the wild, but only with a purpose she can see.
- Builds from varied materials, doable on the spot: found sticks and stones, a store-bought box.
- ⏱ Timed tasks at playgrounds; limitations (eyes closed, one foot, one hand behind the back).
- Maps with readable street names, so she can navigate.

## Dislikes
- Drawing.

## Standing challenges (every trip)
- LEGO passport: collect the stamp at an official LEGO Store in the visited city.

## Getting around
- Walking and public transport; rides on local or historic transport can be challenges.

## Format preferences
- Home city: a campaign of zones conquered in any order over many outings, ~2 zones per full day, one sheet per zone.

## Lessons from past trips
- Every challenge must need being on the spot; a question answerable at home from the card misses the point.
- Too much of a liked kind (LEGO builds, step counting) becomes repetitive; reduce it rather than dropping it.
- Price perks against the trip's coin total and give unspent coins a use (rank on the certificate).
