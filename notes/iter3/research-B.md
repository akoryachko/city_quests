# Research B: paid venues with 2–3 inside challenges (Amsterdam, from Nov 2026)
Researched 2026-10-27. [UNCONFIRMED] marks anything not stated by an official or recent source.

## 1. A'DAM Lookout (Overhoeksplein 5)
Sources: https://www.adamlookout.com/ , https://www.adamlookout.com/en/tickets/ , https://wanderlog.com/place/details/83029
- **Lift:** goes from the 1st to the 20th floor in about 20 s, with light and sound effects and a transparent ceiling (official homepage). Challenge: count down the seconds, or guess the floor number.
- **Top floors (100 m):** an outdoor 360° skydeck plus an indoor observation deck with floor-to-ceiling windows and binoculars (good in bad weather). Also the Madam Skybar & Restaurant.
- **Over the Edge swing:** €7.50 online (€8.50 at the desk), **minimum height 1.20 m**, no minimum age listed. Measure the child beforehand: many 7-year-olds are under 1.20 m.
- **Amsterdam VR Ride:** €7.50 online, also min 1.20 m.
- **Included with every ticket:** a free audio tour (about 30 min) and a free digital souvenir photo.
- **"Amsterdam experience" exhibition on a lower floor:** NOT mentioned on the official site, so treat it as nonexistent or discontinued [UNCONFIRMED].
- **Landmarks:** the official site and reviews mention binoculars/telescopes that "point out historical landmarks", but no source I read names specific ones. That Centraal Station, Eye, NEMO and the ferries are visible is geographically obvious (the tower stands across the IJ from CS, next to the Eye), but it is [UNCONFIRMED by a source].
- **Practical:** daily 10:00–22:00, last entry 21:00. Adult €16.50 online / €18.50 desk. Child (4–12) €10.50 online / €12.50 desk. A family discount exists. Visit length is not stated; plan about 45–60 min (my estimate). Free ferry from behind CS, about 5 min.

## 2. STRAAT Museum (NDSM-Plein 1)
Sources: https://straatmuseum.com/en/plan-your-visit , search snippet of https://straatmuseum.com/en/children-s-treasure-hunt (page now returns 404), https://www.kidsproof.nl/Amsterdam/eropuit/straat-museum/museum-street-art-graffiti-kunst , https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/STRAAT_Museum , https://www.guinnessworldrecords.com/world-records/775459-largest-street-art-gallery , https://www.ndsm.nl/en/nieuws/ndsm-speurtoch-voor-kinderen
- **Kids' treasure hunt:** free, for ages 6–12, including a colouring page, available at reception/ticket desk (STRAAT snippet + Kidsproof, ©2026). It doubles as a poster souvenir. The official page now 404s, so ask at the desk [current availability UNCONFIRMED].
- **Separate NDSM wharf treasure hunt** (outdoor, not STRAAT's): free for primary-school kids. Pick it up at the cafés Pllek, IJver or Noorderlicht, solve the rebus, then return for a treat while supplies last. Current availability is [UNCONFIRMED].
- **Findable artworks:**
  - **Anne Frank portrait by Eduardo Kobra (2016)** on the exterior façade, the museum's "iconic landmark". Free to see outside.
  - **Shepard Fairey murals:** one on the western exterior wall, and a large work inside the permanent collection (2023).
  - Other collection artists (Wikipedia): Cornbread, SJK 171, Buff Monster, Royyal Dog, Guido van Helten, Julio 204, Inkie. Guinness: about 180 works, some up to 15 × 7 m, by Royyal Dog, Guido van Helten, Kobra, Chris Dyer, Farid Rueda, MonkeyBird. Which works hang inside in 2026 is [UNCONFIRMED]; the collection rotates.
  - Count challenge idea: there are 150–180+ works in total, so ask kids to count one hall rather than the whole museum.
- **Practical:** Mon 12–17, Tue–Wed 10–17, Thu 10–21, Fri–Sun 10–17. Closed 5 Nov 2026 and 1 Jan 2027. 24 and 31 Dec close at 17; 25–26 Dec open from 12. Adult **€21.50**, youth 13–18 €11.50, children 0–12 free with an adult (max 5 per adult). Visit duration not stated.

## 3. Amsterdam in Motion (Pazzanistraat 19-23, Zuiveringshal Oost, Westergas)
Sources: https://www.amsterdammuseum.nl/en/tickets-and-visit/locations/amsterdam-in-motion/228553 , https://www.amsterdamtips.com/amsterdam-in-motion , https://www.tiqets.com/en/amsterdam-attractions-c75061/tickets-for-amsterdam-in-motion-audiovisual-show-exhibition-access-p1123624
- **What it is:** run by the Amsterdam Museum and opened Oct 2025 for the city's 750th anniversary. A **200 m² city model at 1:1,300 with 30,500 buildings** ("world's largest multimedia maquette of a city"). A **15-min projection, light and sound show** covers 1275 to the future, in NL and EN, with timed slots. Afterwards you can walk around the model while projections continue; a smaller section bottom-left shows Amsterdam South-East.
- **Upper-floor exhibition:** canals, incl. a **dredged-up bike covered in mussels** (findable!); **Ajax** (mural, shirts, fan corner); **protests, where you can make your own banner**; fashion; "City of change" (interactive); "City of contrasts".
- **Challenge ideas:** find the mussel bike; make a banner; on the model, find your hotel area, the Centraal Station or the Eye. That those landmarks are identifiable on the model is [UNCONFIRMED].
- **Practical:** Mon–Wed 11:30–16:30, Thu–Fri 10–17, Sat–Sun 10–18. Adult €18, **children 0–17 free**, Museumkaart and I amsterdam City Card free. Visit about 45–60 min (amsterdamtips; Tiqets says 1h20). Arrive 15 min before your slot. No café, but Westergas has options.

## 4. Micropia (ARTIS, Plantage Kerklaan)
Sources: https://www.artis.nl/en/artis-micropia , https://rachelsruminations.com/micropia/ (updated May 2024) , https://www.thetripverdict.com/micropia-amsterdam-museum (31 Jan 2026) , https://www.dwell.com/collection/micropia-349951fb
- **Official 2026 items:** Kiss-o-meter; microbe stamp card (from the ticket office); microscopes with living microbes; "discover your own microbiome"; **Microbe of the month**, a findable item that changes monthly; Lab Talks at 11:30, 13:30 and 15:30.
- **Body scanner:** stand in front of a big screen or "digital mirror"; it tracks your arms and shows microbes on body parts (feet, toes, hands, nose). Confirmed in a Jan 2026 blog; not named on the official page.
- **Petri dish wall:** about 5 rows of about 25 dishes, with magnifiers. Two dishes compare a dirty and a clean child's hand. Challenge: find the "hand" dishes and spot the difference. (2024 blog)
- **Rotting food jars:** labelled, dated jars, e.g. a mouldy tomato, Gouda and a cream pie (2024 blog; the 2026 blog mentions a decomposition display with time-lapse of rotting food).
- **Poo display:** a case of bottled animal excrement next to a human digestive system (2024 blog). No source calls it a "poop wall".
- **Dead baby giraffe** decomposed by microbes (2024 blog); **blown-glass microbe sculptures** on top of the disease cabinets (2024 blog); a lab viewing window.
- **Water bear / tardigrade:** live tardigrades under the microscopes (2026 blog). A large tardigrade sculpture in the entrance hall was only in a search snippet of an undated blog; I could not verify it [UNCONFIRMED]. Plush water bears are reportedly in the shop.
- Still current in 2026 per the Jan 2026 blog: body scanner, microscopes, live tardigrades, rotting food. Petri wall, giraffe, poo case and glass microbes come from the 2024 source [likely, UNCONFIRMED for 2026].
- **Practical:** daily 10–17. **Children 0–12 free**, 13+ from €17 online. Weekdays from 16:00 cost €9. Combo with ARTIS: 3–12 from €26.50, 13+ from €35.50. Visit about 90 min (2026 blog).

## 5. Het Scheepvaartmuseum (Kattenburgerplein 1)
Sources: https://hetscheepvaartmuseum.com/whats-on/families-and-children , https://www.hetscheepvaartmuseum.com/whats-on/exhibitions/the-tale-of-the-whale , https://www.kidsproof.nl/Amsterdam/eropuit/het-scheepvaartmuseum/het-verhaal-van-de-walwis-eu-museum-natuur , https://hetscheepvaartmuseum.com/visit/opening-hours-and-admission (via search snippet)
- **The Tale of the Whale:** listed on the museum's current families page, and Kidsproof (©2026) calls it a "vaste tentoonstelling" (permanent). Ages 6+. No end date was found, so it is very likely running in 2026–27, though not explicitly dated. An old museum.nl listing showing "2020, ended" seems outdated.
  - Findables: you **walk into the open mouth of a life-size bowhead (Greenland) whale** at the start, and the **whale's tail plunging into the water** at the end. The final room (with WWF) is about plastic soup. Facts on the page: a blue whale weighs up to 170,000 kg and eats about 3,500 kg of plankton a day. Sperm whale and North Atlantic right whale are also featured.
- **East Indiaman 'Amsterdam' (replica ship):** climb aboard, try the **hammock**, **fire the cannons**, **lift cargo**, take the **helm** and see the **captain's cabin**. Challenge: count the cannons on one deck (the number is not in any source, so the kid finds it).
- **Other:** Doris the Diver (2+), Republiek aan Zee (50 top pieces), navigation instruments (navigating by the stars). A free **Junior Audiotour** about life aboard and whaling. A free **Sea Monsters colouring/activity sheet** at the café.
- **Figureheads hall:** no source I read mentions it [UNCONFIRMED; check on site].
- **Practical:** daily 10–17. Closed King's Day, Christmas Day, New Year's Day. Official admission page (snippet): **children up to 12 free**, 13–17 €8.50, adults €18.50. Card/pin only, no cash. Note the families page itself still says "children up to 4 free", so the sources conflict; the official admission page plus the 2024 price news say ≤12 free. Visit duration not stated.

## 6. NEMO roof (Oosterdok 2), free
Sources: https://www.iamsterdam.com/en/whats-on/calendar/attractions-and-sights/sights/nemos-roof , https://www.nemosciencemuseum.nl/en/press/rooftop-square-nemo-open-to-the-public , https://www.dlr.de/en/media/publications/magazines/all-digital-magazines/dlrmagazine-175/science-at-your-fingertips-in-amsterdam/on-the-nemo-roof-terrace (June 2024) , https://amsterdam750.nl/en/article/a-gift-to-the-city-the-highest-urban-square-in-the-netherlands-opens-on-the-rooftop-of-nemo
- **Status:** redeveloped and reopened 23 May 2025 as a free green rooftop square with 75+ plant species in three biotopes. Access by the stairs on the east side.
- **Current official activities (no named installations):** measure wind speed, study clouds, measure air quality, observe the sun, moon and stars, spot city birds, read sustainability displays, open-air stage.
- **Energetica water cascade** (12 water spouts powered by solar and wind "islands"; make rainbows by pointing the fountain at the sun), sundial, kite: this is from the 2016 exhibit, and a 2024 article still mentions the cascade. Whether it survived the 2025 redesign is **[UNCONFIRMED]**. Water features are probably off or useless in winter.
- **Winter-proof ideas:** measure the wind, name a cloud type, spot birds, and spot landmarks from the high square (the views are a generic fact, not a sourced list).
- **Hours:** iamsterdam says daily 10:00–17:30 (Thu until 21:00 in Jul/Aug). NEMO's museum building was closed on Mondays in Feb–Mar 2026 (museum.nl), so roof hours on Mondays and in winter are [UNCONFIRMED].
