# Research A: Royal Palace, Rijksmuseum, Molen van Sloten, LEGO Store
Researched 2026-10-27. Labels: [OK] = read in a source. [UNCONFIRMED] = conflicting, secondary-only or not found.

## 1. Royal Palace (Koninklijk Paleis), Dam: exterior

### Roof statues (bronze, designed by Artus Quellinus, cast by the Hemony brothers 1663-65)
- Rear roof (Nieuwezijds Voorburgwal side), from left to right: Temperantia, ATLAS, Vigilantia. [OK] Rijksmuseum catalogue entries BK-AM-51-5/-6/-7
  - https://www.rijksmuseum.nl/en/collection/BK-AM-51-6/catalogue-entry
  - https://www.rijksmuseum.nl/en/collection/BK-AM-51-7/catalogue-entry
- Atlas carries a celestial globe ("hemelbol", "the heavens"). He is about 6 m tall (Wikipedia; NOS/DutchNews say 7 m). The globe is about 4 m across and weighs about 1,000 kg (mforamsterdam, a secondary source). [OK] https://nos.nl/l/2529894 , https://mforamsterdam.com/the-palace-on-dam-square/
  - What is drawn on the globe (zodiac or constellations): [UNCONFIRMED]. No source I read describes it, so don't build a question on it.
- Temperantia holds REINS. [OK, secondary only] mforamsterdam
- Vigilantia holds a BOOK and a TORCH, with a ROOSTER at her feet. [OK, secondary only] mforamsterdam. The crane holding a stone does NOT appear in any source I read, so treat it as [UNCONFIRMED/likely wrong]. Use the rooster.
- Front roof (Dam side): Peace (Pax) is in the centre with an olive branch and a caduceus, with a cornucopia at her feet. Justitia is on the LEFT seen from the Dam, with scales and a staff topped by an all-seeing eye. Prudentia is on the RIGHT, with a mirror and a SNAKE. [OK] mforamsterdam. The palace's own PDF confirms Prudentia's attributes (mirror and snake) and her size, 3.8 m tall and almost 3,000 kg.
  - https://www.paleisamsterdam.nl/documents/399/Achtergrond_informatie_Hoog_Kijk_omhoog.pdf
  - Note: the Rijksmuseum catalogue says Prudentia stands at the "left end" and Justitia at the "right end". This is probably described from the building's point of view, so left and right are flipped compared with mforamsterdam. [CONFLICT] Ask the kid "which lady has a snake?" rather than "the left one".
- Roof corners: copper EAGLES hold IMPERIAL CROWNS. The palace PDF says "16 adelaars, 4 keizerskronen", and the crowns are being regilded. [OK] palace PDF

### Atlas restoration status
- The globe came down on 22 Jul 2024. [OK] NOS
- Atlas was back on the roof and complete with his globe by 1 May 2026 (NH Nieuws). The statue is DARK bronze again, but the globe kept its LIGHT GREEN colour. All six roof statues are restored. [OK] https://www.nhnieuws.nl/nieuws/359450/atlas-terug-op-paleis-amsterdam
- On that date Atlas was still surrounded by scaffolding and "barely visible from the street". [OK] Whether the scaffolding is gone by Nov 2026 is [UNCONFIRMED], so check before printing.
  - Nice on-the-spot question if he is visible: "What colour is Atlas, and what colour is his ball?" (dark statue, light-green globe)

### Rear pediment (west tympanum): "The Four Continents pay homage to Amsterdam"
Source: Rijksmuseum model BK-AM-51-3, https://www.rijksmuseum.nl/en/collection/BK-AM-51-3/catalogue-entry [OK]
- In the centre the Maid of Amsterdam sits in front of a COG SHIP, with her feet on a globe. The river gods IJ and Amstel are at her feet.
- Europe brings a horse and oxen. Africa brings a LION, an ELEPHANT and a parrot, plus a chameleon (or salamander). Asia brings a CAMEL, an ostrich and a CROCODILE ridden by a boy. America brings an armadillo and a monkey.
- Goods: bales, a barrel, elephant tusks, tulips and an incense burner.
- Caveat: this describes the terracotta MODEL. The final marble relief is assumed to match closely but I didn't verify it on the building. [partly UNCONFIRMED]

### Front pediment (east tympanum, Dam side): "The sea gods / four world seas pay homage to Amsterdam"
Source: Rijksmuseum model BK-AM-51-2, https://www.rijksmuseum.nl/en/collection/object/De-zeegoden-brengen-hulde-aan-de-stedenmaagd-model-voor-het-timpaan-van-de-voorgevel--14389c7c63dacd075446406ce56f19db [OK]
- The Maid of Amsterdam is enthroned on a throne decorated with LIONS. She wears the imperial crown and has the city shield on her knee.
- Around her are TRITONS riding sea-horses (hippocampi) and DOLPHINS, plus swimming nereids (sea nymphs).
- Neptune is not named in the text I read. [UNCONFIRMED]

### Weathervane on the dome/tower
- A gilded copper COG SHIP, about 2 m tall, dating from about 1664/1665. It sits about 60 m up and is the highest point of the palace. [OK] palace PDF and https://paleisamsterdam.nl/dakrenovatie
- On board are TWO MEN holding flags with three crosses (St Andrew's crosses, as in the city arms) and a SMALL DOG. The ship has NO RUDDER, and the legend says it drifted until it stranded where Amsterdam was founded. The dog's tongue hangs out, but this is "normally invisible at 60 m". [OK] palace PDF
- It was removed in Dec 2023, regilded and is back on top. [OK] dakrenovatie page

### Other things on the street
- On the front balcony is a lion from the Dutch coat of arms, with a crown, sword and arrows. [OK, secondary] mforamsterdam
- Four cast-iron lamp posts stand in front (from 1844) and two more at the back. The ground-floor windows have iron bars. [OK, secondary] mforamsterdam
- The carillon, by Hemony in 1664, plays daily. [OK] palace PDF

### Kid-recognisability (my judgement, not sourced)
- Easy with the naked eye: the golden ship weathervane (it's a ship!). Atlas holding a big ball (if no scaffolding). Peace and the lady with the snake on the front. The golden or green eagles and crowns on the corners.
- Needs binoculars: Vigilantia's rooster and torch, Temperantia's reins, Justitia's scales.
- Needs good binoculars and patience: the pediment animals. The rear relief's elephant, camel and crocodile are good "find the animal" targets, and the front relief has dolphins and sea-horses. These reliefs sit high up in the triangle under the roof.
- Too small or too high: the dog and the men on the weathervane, and anything drawn on Atlas's globe.

## 3. Molen van Sloten (Akersluis 10)
- It is a working polder mill (stellingmolen) with an octagonal top from 1847. It pumps excess rainwater from Amsterdam Nieuw-West to the Ringvaart using an Archimedes' screw (vijzel). [OK] https://www.kidsproof.nl/amsterdam/uitje/molen-van-sloten-molen-van-sloten-rondleiding-familie-uitje , https://molenvansloten.nl/en/technical-data/
- The sails measure 26.40 m across (2 stocks, so 4 sail arms). [OK] technical-data
- The tower is thatched, octagonal and made of oak. [secondary] artessere
- Visit format: a GUIDED TOUR every hour, included in the ticket. It lasts about 1 hour (per visitor reviews). An audio tour is also offered. [OK] iamsterdam, reviews page
  - Special tours for kids 6+ are available on request. Every child gets a colouring page. If you're lucky the miller sets the sails turning. [OK] go-kids, iamsterdam
  - A lift goes up to the stage/gallery (stelling), the outside platform. [OK]
  - The live sail rotation can be watched at https://www.smartmolen.com/mvsloten (handy for a wind check before you go)
- Rembrandt: an ATTIC with life-size/wax FIGURES from Rembrandt's paintings, as a tribute. Rembrandt was a miller's son with ties to Sloten. [OK, secondary] go-kids, listings. Which figures or paintings are shown is [UNCONFIRMED]. This would make a good on-the-spot question: "which painting is this person from?"
- Cooperage (kuiperij) museum: this is a "Kuiperijmuseum" next to or in the mill (named on official site links and Instagram). A 2015 blog says it shows how barrels, casks and wooden buckets were made. [UNCONFIRMED: current state, access and price]
- WINTER HOURS: 2 Nov to 21 Mar, daily 10:00 to 16:00, last tour 15:00. Otherwise 10:00 to 17:00, last tour 16:00. Closed 1 Jan, 27 Apr and 25-26 Dec. [OK] https://www.molenvansloten.nl/
- PRICES (official ticket shop, includes the tour): Adult 18-64 EUR 8.00. Child 4-11 EUR 4.50. Ages 12-17, 65+ and Museumkaart holders EUR 6.75. FREE with I amsterdam City Card or Stadspas. Card payment only at the desk. [OK] https://tickets.molenvansloten.nl/en/tickets (older listings give EUR 7.50/4.00, now outdated)
- Next door: Brouwerij De 7 Deugden (tasting for adults) and Speeltuin Sloten (playground). [OK]
- Kid-countable or nameable items (my suggestions): count the sails (4). Name the screw (Archimedes). Find the Rembrandt figures in the attic. Spot the colouring page. Watch the cap turn.
