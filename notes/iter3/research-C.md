# Research C: playgrounds, staffed ask/order spots, herring, building materials (Amsterdam)

Researched 2026-10-27. Coordinates come from OSM (Nominatim and Overpass, queried today). "OSM eq" means play-equipment nodes mapped in OSM within about 50 m. OSM equipment mapping in Amsterdam is sparse, so an empty list does NOT mean the playground is empty. Tree mapping (natural=tree) is also too sparse to use for "count trees" tasks.

Hours are not published on the sources I could read, and the club site (vereniging-de-noorderspeeltuin.nl) had a certificate error, so hours are UNCONFIRMED. OSM eq: swing.

Open daily 09:00–20:30, possibly earlier closing in winter. OSM shows a playground at Anjeliersstraat (52.37665, 4.87933) with website konippen.nl, which is likely this one (UNCONFIRMED match). ### Zone 9: Westerpark / Westergasfabriek
- **Natuurspeeltuin Het Woeste Westen**, Overbrakerpad 3, 1014 AZ, 52.39072, 4.86608.

  - Activities: sand and water, rafts on ditches, a flowing stream, reeds and bushes, and hut building ("een hut bouwen", per kidsproof).

  - Sources: https://woestewesten.nl/ and https://www.kidsproof.nl/amsterdam/uitje/natuurspeeltuin-het-woeste-westen-het-woeste-westen-speeltuin-kinderen
- **Aldo van Eyck climbing frame ("Klimrek Westerpark")**, by the Haarlemmerweg, 52.38615, 4.87784. OSM eq: climbing frame and 3 jumping stones. Good for "jump all stones X times" or "climb to the top" tasks.

## 2. Staffed places where the kid can ask, translate or order

- **Noordermarkt Saturday markets** (Noordermarkt, 1015 MV; Noorderkerk 52.37963, 4.88640). Source: https://www.iamsterdam.com/zien-en-doen/shoppen/amsterdamse-markten/noordermarkt
  - Saturday 09:00–16:00: organic Boerenmarkt (bread, **cheese**, meat, fish, honey, flowers) plus a general market.
  - The **Lindengracht market** around the corner runs Saturday 09:00–17:00 with 224 stalls (https://www.iamsterdam.com/zien-en-doen/shoppen/amsterdamse-markten/markt-lindengracht).
  - Monday 09:00–14:00: Westerstraat textiles/antiques market (no food focus).
  - Cheese stall name: NOT FOUND. Sources say there is cheese, including raw-milk and goat cheese, and "lots of free nibbles". One 2006 blog mentions free cheese samples, which is dated. Asking "hoe oud is de oudste kaas?" is plausible but unconfirmed.
- **Winkel 43**, Noordermarkt 43, 52.37908, 4.88623. Famous for apple pie with whipped cream (appeltaart met slagroom).
  - Hours per iamsterdam: Mon 07:00–01:00, Sat 07:00–03:00, Sun 09:00–01:00. The own site says it opens at 07:00 on Saturdays.
  - No reservations, so expect queues on market days. Price not found.
  - Sources: https://www.iamsterdam.com/nl/zien-en-doen/eten-en-drinken/alle-restaurants/winkel-43 and https://winkel43.nl/en
- **Albert Cuypmarkt**, Albert Cuypstraat, 1073 BD. Open Mon–Sat 09:00–18:00 per iamsterdam (another source says 17:00), closed Sunday. Source: https://www.iamsterdam.com/en/see-and-do/shopping/amsterdam-markets/albert-cuypmarkt
  - **Rudi's Original Stroopwafels**, Albert Cuypstraat ~180/182. The street centre near there is about 52.3547, 4.8893 (Nominatim gave street level only).
    - About €2.50 per fresh stroopwafel (DutchNews Feb 2024); a later blog says €2.50–3.50.
    - Mon–Sat about 10:00–17:00 per blog.
    - The kid can order "één verse stroopwafel, alstublieft".
    - Sources: https://dutchnews.nl/2024/02/e13-for-a-stroopwafel-is-this-the-most-expensive-tourist-snack and https://www.theinfatuation.com/amsterdam/reviews/rudis-stroopwafels
  - **Vishandel Albert Cuyp**, Albert Cuypstraat 155. Fish stall on the market for 65+ years (iamsterdam best-herring page).
- **Hortus Botanicus cash desk**, Plantage Middenlaan 2A, 52.36681, 4.90778. Open daily 10:00–17:00 (closed 25 Dec and 1 Jan). Adult €14.75, child 5–17 €8.50, under 4 free. **Card only.** Source: https://www.dehortus.nl/en/plan-your-visit/

## 3. Herring
- **Stubbe's Haring**, Singel 8n, 1013 GA (at the Haarlemmersluis, where Brouwersgracht meets Singel), 52.37906, 4.89424.
  - Hours: **Tue–Sat 10:00–18:00, closed Sun and Mon**. Restaurantguru, amsterdam-explorer and mindtrip agree; one Weeblyte page says it closes around 17:30. So a **Saturday in November works; Sunday does not.**
  - Herring is served on wax paper and best eaten at the cart (iamsterdam).
  - Price: blogs give about €3–4 for a herring and about €5 for a broodje. Unofficial and possibly dated.
  - Sources: https://restaurantguru.com/Stubbes-Haring-Amsterdam, https://amsterdam-explorer.com/herring/, https://www.iamsterdam.com/en/see-and-do/restaurant-and-bars/best-herring-in-amsterdam, https://www.eatingeurope.com/where-to-eat-herring/
- **Vishandel 't Centrum**, Haarlemmerdijk 4, 1013 JC, 52.38172, 4.88904. Small shop. Herring is cleaned in front of you and you can eat at the bar (iamsterdam). Hours per openingstijden.nl: Tue–Fri 08:00–18:00, Sat 08:00–17:00, closed Sun/Mon (page may be dated). https://www.openingstijden.nl/Vishandel-Centrum/Amsterdam/Haarlemmerdijk-4/
- **Sunday fallback: Frens Haringhandel**, Koningsplein/Singel (by the Flower Market), 52.36729, 4.88944. Hours 11:00–17:00: iamsterdam says daily, amsterdam-explorer says closed Tuesday. It is not in the Jordaan. https://www.iamsterdam.com/uit/agenda/winkelen/winkels/haringhandel-frens
- **Year-round?** Hollandse Nieuwe arrives mid-June, but "signs appear all year" (iamsterdam love-letter article). The stalls above list no winter closure, but November availability is UNCONFIRMED.
- **Ordering vocabulary** (iamsterdam): "broodje haring" is herring in a soft roll with optional *uitjes* (onions) and *zuur* (pickles). "Haring met uitjes" is herring by hand without a roll. Source: https://www.iamsterdam.com/en/see-and-do/restaurant-and-bars/love-letter-to-the-herring-stalls-of-amsterdam
- **Herring and bun separately so the kid assembles it**: NO source confirms a stand will sell a loose bun ("een los broodje"). Suggested script: the kid asks "Mag ik een haring met uitjes en zuur, en een broodje apart?" Fallback: order herring with onions and pickles on the tray and bring a soft white bun (zacht wit bolletje) from a bakery or supermarket. UNCONFIRMED.
- **Bench snack from supermarket ingredients** (sources are blogs and Wikipedia, not academic):
  - **Hagelslag on buttered bread** is the most iconic and easiest. About 750,000 slices a day are eaten, and RIVM says 1 in 5 sandwiches gets chocolate spread or hagelslag. https://www.iamexpat.nl/lifestyle/lifestyle-news/brief-history-dutch-sprinkles-aka-hagelslag
  - **Ontbijtkoek with a thin layer of butter** is common, and Peijnenburg is the best-known brand (search summary, transparent.com Dutch blog). Easy to do with a plastic knife.
  - **Beschuit met muisjes** (rusk with aniseed comfits) is tied to births: it is served to visitors when a baby is born. It is iconic but fiddly on a bench because the rusk crumbles and the muisjes roll off, and it needs butter as glue. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muisjes
  - Recommendation: hagelslag on a buttered bolletje, or ontbijtkoek with butter.

## 4. Natural building materials (sticks, leaves, pinecones)
- **Het Woeste Westen** (Westerpark): hut building is listed as an activity (kidsproof). The rules page (woestewesten.nl/regels) returned 404. On-site collecting rules are UNCONFIRMED, so ask the supervisor. Using materials already on the grounds seems to be the point of the place.
- **Amsterdamse Bos Speeleilanden**: "ruimte om hutten te bouwen" (room to build huts) per kidsproof, and iamsterdam lists "hut-building". This is the best spot for a build-on-the-spot task.
- **Amsterdamse Bos, general**:
  - The official kids/playground pages say nothing about collecting fallen material.
  - The official site mentions chestnut hunting in autumn at the Doolhof by the goat farm, which implies picking up chestnuts there is accepted.
  - The municipality prosecuted a man for taking 68 mushrooms (theft and poaching), so **do not pick mushrooms**.
- **General Dutch norm** (news articles, not law texts): "Officieel mag je niets meenemen uit het bos" (officially you may take nothing out of the forest). A small amount, about 250 g (a few chestnuts or branches), is tolerated. Branches, moss and leaves should not be taken "zomaar" (casually). Protected moss can bring a fine.
  - **Safe framing for the quest**: build with fallen material on the spot and leave it there. Don't take anything home and don't break living branches.
  - Sources: https://www.nhnieuws.nl/nieuws/326653/is-kastanjes-rapen-in-het-bos-stelen-nh-zoekt-uit-dit-mag-je-meenemen-uit-het-bos and https://nos.nl/artikel/2448747-boswachters-neem-niet-te-veel-kastanjes-en-paddenstoelen-mee-uit-het-bos
- No Amsterdam city-park (APV) rule specifically about picking up fallen leaves or sticks was found: UNCONFIRMED.
